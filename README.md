# Queenture
Queenture is a modern e-commerce platform designed for selling furniture online. The website provides a seamless shopping experience where users can browse a wide range of furniture products, add items to their cart, and securely complete purchases.

✨ Key Features
User-Friendly Interface: Clean and intuitive design for easy navigation.

Product Catalog: Organized furniture listings with detailed descriptions and images.

Shopping Cart & Checkout: Smooth process for adding items and completing orders.

Responsive Design: Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Secure Transactions: Integrated payment options to ensure safe purchases.

🎯 Purpose
The goal of Queenture is to make online furniture shopping simple, reliable, and enjoyable. It empowers customers to explore and buy furniture from the comfort of their homes
